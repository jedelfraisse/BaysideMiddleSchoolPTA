# PTA Budget & Financial Information (draft)

> **Draft Notice**  
> This document is a preliminary draft prepared for discussion with the PTA group.  
> All dates, activities, and details are subject to revision based on feedback  
> from PTA members, volunteers, and school leadership.

The `budget/` folder contains financial information, planning notes, and reports related to the Bayside Middle School PTA. These documents help maintain transparency, support decision‑making, and ensure responsible use of PTA funds.

## Contents
- **Annual Budget Draft**  
  A working draft of expected income and expenses for the 2026–2027 school year.

- **Treasurer Reports**  
  Summaries of current balances, recent transactions, and upcoming financial needs.

- **Event Cost Estimates**  
  Draft cost breakdowns for proposed PTA events and student challenges.

- **Funding Requests**  
  Notes or forms related to teacher or staff requests for PTA support.

## Purpose of This Folder
- Provide a clear financial picture for PTA members  
- Support planning for events, challenges, and enrichment activities  
- Maintain organized records for future PTA boards  
- Ensure transparency and accountability

## Notes
All budget documents are drafts until reviewed and approved by the PTA group and Treasurer. Updates will be made throughout the school year as events are finalized and expenses occur.
