# PTA Meetings (Agendas & Minutes)

This folder contains all PTA meeting agendas and minutes for the 2026–2027 school year. Each meeting is documented with two files:

- **meeting-YYYY-MM-DD-agenda.md** — The agenda used to guide the meeting  
- **meeting-YYYY-MM-DD-minutes.md** — The official minutes recorded during or after the meeting  

Using a consistent naming format keeps meetings organized chronologically and makes it easy for parents, volunteers, and future PTA board members to review past discussions, decisions, and action items.

## Structure
- Agendas and minutes are stored together in this folder  
- Files use the ISO date format (YYYY-MM-DD) for clear sorting  
- Each meeting has exactly two files for clarity and compliance  

## Purpose
PTA agendas and minutes are important for:
- Transparency  
- Record‑keeping  
- Continuity between school years  
- Supporting future PTA leadership  
- Providing parents with insight into PTA activities and decisions  

This folder ensures that all meeting documentation is easy to find, easy to read, and preserved year‑to‑year.
