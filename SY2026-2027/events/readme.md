# PTA Events Overview (draft)

> **Draft Notice**  
> This document is a preliminary draft prepared for discussion with the PTA group.  
> All dates, activities, and details are subject to revision based on feedback  
> from PTA members, volunteers, and school leadership.

The `events/` folder contains information about PTA‑supported activities, family engagement nights, House‑based events, and school community programs planned for the 2026–2027 school year. These events help strengthen school spirit, support the RCA House System, and build connections between families, students, and staff.

## Purpose of This Folder
- Provide a central place for all PTA event planning documents  
- Outline proposed events for the school year  
- Support coordination between PTA members, volunteers, and school staff  
- Offer draft descriptions and timelines for review and refinement  
- Maintain organized records for future PTA boards  

## Types of Events Included
- **Family Engagement Events**  
  Examples: Game Night, Movie Night, Food Truck Night, Paint Night, Line Dancing

- **House‑Based Events**  
  Examples: Trivia Night, STEM Challenge Night, Art Night, House Olympics

- **Seasonal Events**  
  Examples: Fall Festival, Winter Craft Night, Spring Carnival

- **Signature Events**  
  Examples: House Festival, Reflections Showcase, End‑of‑Year Celebration

- **School Support Events**  
  Examples: Open House table, Staff Appreciation Week, Volunteer Recruitment

## What You’ll Find Here
- Draft descriptions of proposed events  
- Tentative dates and planning notes  
- Cost estimates (linked from the budget folder)  
- Volunteer needs and staffing notes  
- Event templates for future reuse  

## Notes
All event documents in this folder are drafts and will be updated as planning progresses and feedback is received from the PTA group and school leadership.
