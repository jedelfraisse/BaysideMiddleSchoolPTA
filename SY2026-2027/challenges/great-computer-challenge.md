The Great Computer Challenge is a regional student competition that encourages creativity, problem‑solving, and technology skills. Bayside Middle School has participated in past years, and the PTA can help support student involvement for 2026–2027.

## Overview
The GCC offers multiple categories such as:
- Graphic Design  
- Video Editing  
- Desktop Publishing  
- Programming  
- Web Design  
- Robotics (depending on grade level)

Students typically compete in teams, and the event is hosted by WHRO and Old Dominion University.

## Goals for Bayside
- Increase student awareness of GCC opportunities  
- Support teams with practice sessions and materials  
- Encourage participation across all RCA Houses  
- Highlight student achievements at school events or assemblies  

## Timeline (Tentative)
- **October:** Interest form shared with students  
- **November:** Team formation and category selection  
- **December–March:** Practice sessions and skill development  
- **April:** GCC competition event  
- **May:** Recognition of participants at House Festival or end‑of‑year events  

## PTA Support
- Help coordinate practice times  
- Provide materials (USB drives, art supplies, headphones, etc.)  
- Recruit parent volunteers with tech experience  
- Assist with transportation or supervision on event day

Dates and categories will be updated once official GCC information is released for 2026–2027.
