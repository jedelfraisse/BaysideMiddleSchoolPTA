# Student Challenges & Enrichment (draft)

> **Draft Notice**  
> This document is a preliminary draft prepared for discussion with the PTA group.  
> All dates, activities, and details are subject to revision based on feedback  
> from PTA members, volunteers, and school leadership.

The `challenges/` folder contains information about student enrichment opportunities supported by the Bayside Middle School PTA. These programs encourage creativity, academic growth, teamwork, and engagement across all RCA Houses.

## Included Programs
- **Reflections Program**  
  National PTA arts competition with the 2026–2027 theme: *“What My Culture Means to Me.”*

- **Great Computer Challenge (GCC)**  
  Regional technology competition offering categories such as graphic design, programming, video editing, and more.

- **STEM Challenges**  
  Hands‑on engineering, coding, robotics, and science activities that can be run as House events or school‑wide enrichment.

## Purpose of This Folder
- Provide clear, parent‑friendly descriptions of each challenge  
- Outline tentative timelines for planning  
- Support coordination between PTA, staff, and volunteers  
- Encourage participation across all RCA Houses  
- Serve as a reference for future school years

## Notes
All challenge documents in this folder are drafts and will be updated as more information becomes available from Virginia PTA, WHRO/ODU, and Bayside Middle School staff.
