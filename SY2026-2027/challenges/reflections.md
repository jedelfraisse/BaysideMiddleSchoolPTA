> This document is a working draft for discussion with the PTA group.  
> All dates, activities, and details will be updated based on feedback  
> from PTA members, volunteers, and school leadership.



The Reflections Program is a national arts contest sponsored by the PTA. Students in Pre‑K through grade 12 create original works based on an annual theme and may submit entries in multiple artistic categories. Participation is open to all Bayside Middle School students through our PTA.

## 2026–2027 Theme
**“What My Culture Means to Me”**

This theme invites students to explore identity, heritage, traditions, and the cultural influences that shape who they are. Entries may reflect personal experiences, family stories, community traditions, or broader cultural expressions.

## Categories
Students may submit entries in:
- Dance Choreography  
- Film Production  
- Literature  
- Music Composition  
- Photography  
- Visual Arts  
- Accessible Arts (for students with disabilities)

## Grade Divisions
- Primary (PreK–2)  
- Intermediate (3–5)  
- Middle School (6–8)  
- High School (9–12)  
- Accessible Arts (all grades)

## How Reflections Works (Virginia PTA Summary)
- Students submit entries to the **local school PTA** in the fall.  
- Bayside PTA selects winners for each category and grade division.  
- First‑place entries move on to the **City/County Council PTA** (if applicable).  
- Council winners advance to **Virginia PTA** for state‑level judging.  
- State winners advance to **National PTA**.  
- Awards and celebrations occur at the council, state, and national levels throughout winter and spring.

## Virginia PTA Timeline (Tentative)
These dates come from Virginia PTA’s published schedule and may shift slightly:

- **Oct/Nov:** Students submit entries to Bayside PTA  
- **Mid‑December:** Bayside PTA submits first‑place entries to Council PTA  
- **Jan 11:** Deadline for regional submissions (for PTAs *not* in a council)  
- **Jan 25:** Council winners due to Virginia PTA  
- **Feb 28:** Virginia PTA announces state winners  
- **Mid‑March:** Virginia PTA Student Awards Celebration  
- **May 1:** National PTA announces national winners  

## House Reflections Challenge (Bayside Idea)
To encourage participation across the RCA House System, Houses can earn points for:
- Number of submissions  
- House‑wide participation  
- Creativity and interpretation of the theme  
- Special recognition categories (e.g., “Most Heartfelt,” “Most Original”)  

This approach ties Reflections into the House System and promotes friendly, meaningful engagement.

## PTA Support
- Provide materials (paper, art supplies, etc.)  
- Host after‑school or virtual “creation sessions”  
- Recruit judges (non‑PTA members, per program rules)  
- Display student work at school or online  
- Help families understand submission guidelines and forms  
- Coordinate submission packaging and digital uploads for Council/State
