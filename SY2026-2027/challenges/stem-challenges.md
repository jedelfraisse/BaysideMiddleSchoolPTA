# STEM Challenges (draft)

> **Draft Notice**  
> This document is a working draft for discussion with the PTA group.  
> All dates, activities, and details will be updated based on feedback  
> from PTA members, volunteers, and school leadership.

STEM Challenges offer students hands‑on opportunities to explore science, technology, engineering, and math through creative problem‑solving. These activities can be run as House events, classroom extensions, or PTA‑supported enrichment opportunities.

## Overview
STEM challenges may include:
- Engineering builds (bridges, towers, catapults)  
- Coding puzzles  
- Robotics mini‑tasks  
- Science experiments  
- Math‑based logic challenges  

These activities can be scaled for grade level and time available.

## Goals for Bayside
- Support RCA House System with fun, educational challenges  
- Encourage teamwork and critical thinking  
- Provide low‑cost, high‑impact enrichment opportunities  
- Build excitement around STEM fields  

## Event Ideas
### House STEM Challenge Night
- Teams compete in timed engineering or science tasks  
- Points awarded for creativity, stability, accuracy, or speed  

### Monthly Mini‑Challenges
- Quick 20–30 minute challenges during House meetings  
- Examples: paper airplane distance, marshmallow tower, coding maze  

### STEM Fair (Spring)
- Students display projects or inventions  
- Houses earn points for participation and creativity  

## Timeline (Tentative)
- **October:** First House STEM mini‑challenge  
- **January:** STEM Challenge Night  
- **March:** Coding or robotics challenge  
- **April/May:** STEM Fair or showcase  

## PTA Support
- Provide materials (tape, cups, popsicle sticks, paper, etc.)  
- Recruit volunteers to help run stations  
- Assist with judging and scoring  
- Help promote events through ParentSquare and flyers  

## Notes
Specific challenges and dates will be finalized with input from school staff and the principal.
